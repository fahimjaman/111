## Current Graduate Research Projects

### Automated Track and Overhead Contact System Inspection Using UAS and LiDAR for Maryland Light RailLink

Research involving unmanned aerial systems and LiDAR-based inspection workflows for rail transportation infrastructure.

### Road Space Allocation for Complete Streets

Research focused on roadway space allocation and multimodal street design, with emphasis on accessibility, safety, and equitable use of transportation infrastructure.

### Highway Safety Manual and Crash Modification Factors

Research related to transportation safety analysis, crash evaluation, safety performance, and evidence-based countermeasure assessment.

## Undergraduate Research

### Motorcycle E-Ride Hailing Services in Bangladesh by Analyzing Structural Equation Modelling Approach

A questionnaire survey of 300 respondents in Dhaka City was used to examine motorcycle ride-hailing service quality, limitations, user characteristics, safety, economic factors, and management factors using Structural Equation Modeling.
