## Research

My research program focuses on improving transportation systems through data-driven analysis, emerging technologies, and interdisciplinary methods.

### Research Areas

- Public Transportation & Shared-Use Mobility
- Traffic Safety & Accident Analysis
- Driver & Pedestrian Behavior
- Highway Geometric Design & Traffic Operations
- Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)
- Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs)
- Non-Motorized Transportation
- Transportation Equity
- Transportation Planning & Accessibility
- UAS and LiDAR Applications

### Current Graduate Research Projects

**Automated Track and Overhead Contact System Inspection Using UAS and LiDAR for Maryland Light RailLink**  
Research involving unmanned aerial systems and LiDAR-based inspection workflows for rail infrastructure.

**Road Space Allocation for Complete Streets**  
Research on allocating roadway space among different users to support safer, more accessible multimodal streets.

**Highway Safety Manual and Crash Modification Factors**  
Research related to quantitative roadway safety analysis and evidence-based countermeasure evaluation.

### Methods & Analytical Tools

**Methods:** Structural Equation Modeling (SEM), Difference-in-Differences, Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA), statistical modeling, machine learning, transportation simulation.

**Programming:** C++, MATLAB, Python.

**Software:** ArcGIS, AutoCAD, ETABS, SPSS, IBM SPSS Amos, SCARM, Google Earth Pro, Code::Blocks, Microsoft Office.
